# Company Brain by Deliberate

Your team's Claudes, one memory. Anything one person saves from their Claude, everyone's Claude can find: decisions, answers, how-tos.

## What it does

- **Searches first.** Ask Claude about your company, its customers, products, vendors, policies or past decisions, and it searches your team's Company Brain before answering. It tells you who saved what it found, and when.
- **Saves what you settle.** When a conversation settles a decision, an answer or a how-to, Claude saves it to the brain and tells you in one line. Claude can correct an item for you, and owners and admins can delete any item from the Library.
- **Never saves** personal matters, small talk, drafts in progress, anything you call private or off the record, credentials, or customers' personal details.

## Before you start

You need a Company Brain from Deliberate: the Brain plan ($249/month, 14 days free, through the Shopify App Store), or the Agent Team, where every agent reads and writes the same memory. https://deliberatestudio.com/company-brain. Each person connects once: type your workspace name (the first part of yourteam.deliberatestudio.com) and sign in with the email your team invited. Setup steps: https://deliberatestudio.com/company-brain/connect. Docs: https://deliberatestudio.com/claude

If your team already installed its own Company Brain plugin from its brain's page, use that one or this one, not both, or Claude will see the same tools twice.

## What this plugin runs, sends and fetches

This plugin has no code, scripts or hooks. It adds two skills (instructions for Claude) and one connector: Deliberate's connector at https://mcp.deliberatestudio.com/mcp, which each person signs in to themselves. People connected at the older address, https://deliberatestudio.com/api/brain/mcp, keep working. If you update this plugin, Claude asks you to sign in once more.

Deliberate receives only what Claude searches for (the words searched, who searched, what was found) and what it saves or updates through that connector. Deliberate can't read your Claude conversations, files or account. Your team can see the searches, and Deliberate staff may review them to support you and to recommend our other services. We never sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI models. Searches are kept up to 400 days, and saved items until you delete them.
